publish a third note that demonstrates how he learns an unfamiliar domain by
building. Both additions should reinforce the Living Notebook narrative rather
than make the site feel like a promotional portfolio.

## Approaches considered

1. **Approved: editorial portrait plus a full third note.** Uses the real
   photograph without fighting its aspect ratio and gives the simulator its own
   learning narrative. This best supports the site's professional, hands-on
   positioning.
2. Crop the talk photograph into a wide banner and leave the simulator only as
   a project. Rejected because the crop weakens the photograph and loses a
   useful example of learning in public.
3. Keep the typographic talk fallback and publish only the note. Rejected
   because the approved photograph is stronger evidence than the placeholder.
